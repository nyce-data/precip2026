Event at a glance

Two half days. No talks. Participants score datasets and map who depends on them, and then the organizing team narrows the list.

Day 1 · Tuesday, December 15

Score

Participants score each precipitation dataset for risk and usefulness with the rubric from the pre-read. They score what they know and flag what they don't.

Day 2 · Wednesday, December 16

Map

Participants map each dataset from its source to the people who depend on it: who measures, curates, funds, and uses it.

Afterward, the organizing team narrows the list to a handful of datasets for follow-up and builds proposals to broaden their funding base. Precipitation is the pilot. The method becomes a template for other fields. Discussion of specific datasets stays within the group.
